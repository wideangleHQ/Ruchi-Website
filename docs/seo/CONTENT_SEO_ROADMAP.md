## B. Content opportunities (priority order)
Each item lists the 10 requested attributes compactly. "Needs" = information/approval required before writing.

### 2. Recipes tied to products (shoppable)
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Topics | Chana masala *(exists)*, biryani, chicken curry, dal tadka, sambar, chaat, dalma |
| Intent | "<dish> recipe" → informational, then buy |
| Titles / slugs | "Chicken Biryani at Home with Ruchi Biryani Masala" → `/blog/recipes/chicken-biryani` |
| Primary / supporting keywords | `<dish> recipe` / `<dish> masala`, `how to make <dish>` |
| Outline | Intro, ingredients, steps, timing, serving notes, "Shop this recipe" (the recipe-products field is ready in Shopify) |
| Internal links | Recipe ↔ product pages ↔ collection |
| Priority | **High** |
| Needs | Tested recipes written by the business (no invented quantities/times) and the product links |

### 3. Individual spice guides (e.g. "Turmeric (haldi): how to use it in everyday cooking")
Intent informational; slug `/blog/spice-guide/turmeric-haldi`; primary `turmeric powder uses` (hypothesis); outline: what it is, forms RUCHI sells (powder), how it's used in cooking, storage (**use the product's own storage text**), links to PDP + Basic Spices. Priority Medium. Needs: approval; **no health claims** without substantiation.

### 5. Distributor enquiry FAQ
Real questions only. Source them from actual distributor conversations. Needs: terms, territories, onboarding steps.

### 6. Export information
Intent: "indian spice export supplier". Build on `/global-business` with only verified markets, certifications and documentation. Needs: owner-verified facts.

### 7. Brand heritage / manufacturing
Expand `/about` with a short factual summary near the top (who, where, since when, what) in addition to the long story. Needs: owner confirmation of manufacturing facts and the "100% pure" and ISO statements already on the page.

### 8. Product education on PDPs
Add a short "How to use" line per product **only from the business's own product copy**. Needs: copy.
